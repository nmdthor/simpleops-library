Weekly small-business checklist

Set aside 30–45 minutes each week. Copy this list into your notes and check off each item.

Money

  • Record all sales and expenses.
  • Check unpaid invoices and upcoming bills.
  • Review cash available for the next two weeks.

Customers

  • Respond to unanswered customer questions.
  • Identify recurring complaints or requests.
  • Follow up on promises made last week.

Operations

  • Check supplies and reorder only what is needed.
  • Review the biggest time-consuming task.
  • Choose one improvement for next week.

Next week

Write your three most important priorities and assign each a due date.
