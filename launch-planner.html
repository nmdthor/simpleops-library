30-day small-project launch planner

Days 1–7: Validate

Define one customer group and one problem. Interview potential customers or examine credible evidence of demand. Write a simple offer.

Days 8–14: Build

Create the smallest useful version. Draft a clear page describing what it does, for whom, and what it costs.

Days 15–21: Test

Ask a small group to try it. Watch where they get stuck. Improve the most confusing part first.

Days 22–30: Launch and learn

Publish the offer, share it in relevant communities where allowed, track visits and conversions, and review feedback before expanding.

Measure real demand; do not assume early revenue.
